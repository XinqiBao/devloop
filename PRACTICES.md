# Practices

These are defaults to evaluate, not universal rules. Current user and project intent, including existing constraints, takes precedence.

- **Understand relevant reality.** Before consequential changes, inspect the actual behavior, instructions, worktree, dependencies, and affected boundaries. Scale the investigation to uncertainty, reversibility, and impact.
- **Preserve intent.** Keep a change within the requested outcome and existing contracts. Surface materially different choices instead of silently replacing specific direction with a generic process.
- **Ground claims in evidence.** Verify observable behavior at the least costly boundary that captures the risk. Report what ran, what did not, and what remains uncertain; a passing command or self-review alone may not prove the intended outcome.
- **Keep guidance scoped and current.** Put a durable rule where its audience will encounter it, without duplicating it across user, project, and tool layers. Keep temporary state and private machine facts out of public guidance.
- **Prefer native capability and judgment.** Add skills, hooks, scripts, or dependencies only for recurring problems they solve better than existing agent behavior or concise instructions, with a credible way to maintain and verify them.
- **Act within authority.** Proceed on clear, in-scope work; escalate material changes to data, security, architecture, user-owned configuration, or external state when the choice is not already authorized. Do not turn a routine task into a fixed ceremony.
- **Promote deliberately, prune regularly.** Distill a recurring, evidenced failure into the smallest useful practice. Remove or demote artifacts when their need disappears or native capability overtakes them.
