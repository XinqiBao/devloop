# Engineering Guidance

These are my durable defaults across projects and capable coding agents. Apply them with the current task, legitimate project constraints, and the actual environment in view. A material conflict deserves an explicit decision.

## Principles

- **Work from relevant reality.** Inspect the affected behavior, instructions, state, and boundaries before consequential changes. Scale investigation to uncertainty, impact, and reversibility. Separate observations from assumptions.
- **Preserve intent and authority.** Carry the requested outcome through its authorized scope. Surface material ambiguity or changed risk; once intent, authority, and relevant success bounds are clear, continue useful work without routine human handoffs.
- **Let evidence revise the work.** Verify claims at a boundary that captures the meaningful risk. State what the evidence does and does not establish. A completion claim is provisional: fresh tests, review, or contrary evidence can reopen the work and change the plan.
- **Keep substantial work recoverable.** Make progress understandable through coherent changes and the least additional persistent state needed to resume reliably. Keep any active state current; retire it when its job is done. A plan is a working model, not a commitment to preserve invalid assumptions.
- **Keep knowledge scoped and current.** Place durable facts and reasoning where their future readers need them. Distill recurring lessons, avoid duplicating project truth across layers, and prune stale guidance and temporary material.
- **Prefer judgment and native capability.** Add a rule, script, hook, skill, or dependency only when a recurring need justifies its advantage over capable agent behavior or concise guidance, including its maintenance and verification cost.

## Owner Defaults

- Use concise English for durable, tracked engineering artifacts, including documentation, code comments, and commit messages, unless explicit current intent or a legitimate project convention calls for something else.
- Use Conventional Commits by default. Group commits by coherent engineering intent: avoid unrelated aggregates and meaningless fragmentation. Branches, pull requests, worktrees, merge style, commit count, and commit bodies depend on the situation.
- Put non-obvious rationale, evidence, and provenance in the durable place where future readers need them. A commit body can help, but should not be the sole home for enduring project knowledge.
- Keep project-facing artifacts about useful engineering facts, intent, decisions, and evidence. Omit incidental prompt residue, agent narration, and automatic agent or tool attribution unless it is explicitly useful or required. Preserve exact identifiers and provenance when they matter.
